Parlomino stands on the shoulders of open projects. Thank you to their authors.

## Pictograms adapted from OpenMoji

Many objects, foods, animals, places, body parts, colors and some feelings are adapted from
[OpenMoji](https://openmoji.org), the open-source emoji and icon project, under the
[CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/) license.

> All emojis designed by [OpenMoji](https://openmoji.org/) – the open-source emoji and icon project. License: [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/)

**Changes made by Parlomino:** colors mapped to the Parlomino palette (shades merged into flat colors),
outlines thickened, hands and body parts recolored in skin tone, made recolorable for the high-contrast theme.
This does not imply any endorsement by the OpenMoji project.

Every downloaded SVG contains its own credit, and the engine returns the credits of every pictogram it composes,
so that apps can display them.

## Pictograms drawn for Parlomino

People and actions are drawn with the Parlomino character, and some concepts by hand, for this project.
They are original works under CC BY-SA 4.0. They follow principles shared by AAC symbol sets
(show a person doing the action, close-ups for the face and hands) without copying any of their drawings.
